Who We Are?

Coal fuelled the drive of the Industrial Revolution, bringing power to our lives in the form of electricity, transport and the present order of the world, as we now know it. S.A.S. TRADING is a supplier of a wide range of high-quality coal products to various sectors in Eastern & Central India.

Company Profile

What we are proud of

S.A.S. TRADING, introduces itself as a supplier of wide range of coal. We are one dependable name when it comes to supplying high quality coal. We are engaged in catering to the diversified needs of various sectors throughout the Eastern and Central part of India covering Trade Hubs, Steel Mills, Tea Factories etc.


Our recipe for sourcing of Coal is Quality and Cost-effective product with minimum time. S.A.S. TRADING, believe that environment is the most beautiful asset we have. Keeping this in mind, we suggest clients clean Coal with very low ash content, and low sulphur and nitrogen content rather than coal producing more pollution. With a mission to understand buyer’s needs and fulfil their requirements, we supply high quality coal at competitive prices. For us, all buyers are the same and we provide standard services to all, regardless of value or volume of the business we do with them. We make sure to be available for our clients round the clock and strive to deliver fast and safe supply of our products. We at S.A.S.TRADING have set new paradigms of authenticity that is unsurpassable.
